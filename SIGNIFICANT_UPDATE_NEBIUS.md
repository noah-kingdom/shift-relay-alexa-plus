# Significant Update Statement

SHIFT//RELAY previously demonstrated durable operational continuity and evidence-gated action control in an Alexa+/MCP context. During the Nebius/NVIDIA submission period, it was substantially extended into an evidence-grounded autonomous operations architecture: NVIDIA Nemotron running through Nebius Token Factory now performs live decomposition of frontline natural-language reports into typed operational issues; Tavily performs live external evidence retrieval; and SHIFT//RELAY deterministically controls whether those reported claims may transition to VERIFIED state and whether high-risk actions may become available. The new runtime path, evidence policy, and state-dependent action authority are functional changes to the core behavior, not a cosmetic rebrand.
